I see painting as a way to freeze time in the face of my surroundings, whether in the lushness of a garden or the architecture of the city. I usually work outdoors (plein air), moving my easel to capture the fleeting light and atmosphere of each place—a process that then continues in the calm of the studio.

Each oil brushstroke seeks to convey onto the canvas the real atmosphere and truth of what lies before me.
