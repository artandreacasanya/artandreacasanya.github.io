Plein air

My process begins outside the studio. Working outdoors allows me to capture the real light, the atmosphere of the place, and the immediate energy of the surroundings. At the easel, I record the first strokes, the live color, and the essential structure of the scene.

The Studio

Back in the studio, the process shifts gears. It is a space for pause, reflection, and technical development. In the studio, I resume the work I began outdoors to refine the composition and work on the material and layers more calmly, until I find the piece’s final balance.
