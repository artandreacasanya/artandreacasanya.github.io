Shipping Information

Each artwork is shipped insured and individually packaged from the studio. Shipping costs vary by destination and are confirmed before the purchase is finalized:

  • Domestic shipping (mainland Spain): included in the price.
  • Shipping to the Balearic and Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla: please inquire.
  • International shipping: please inquire. Any customs duties are the responsibility of the buyer.

If you have any questions about shipping to your destination before deciding, write to me and I will send you an exact quote:

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the shipping costs?

Shipping is included within mainland Spain. For the islands and other countries, please ask for a quote.

Do the works come with a certificate of authenticity?

Yes, every original work comes with a signed certificate of authenticity confirming that it is a unique piece.

How can I pay for a work?

For the time being, payment is made by bank transfer once the availability of the work has been confirmed through the contact email (art.andreacasanya@gmail.com).

How can I see which works are available?

In the main gallery you can see every piece in each series, and use the availability counter and the filters button to browse the works by availability, price or date.

Do you take commissions?

As a rule I work on my own series and landscapes, which I paint and add to the website, but if you have a specific idea in mind you are welcome to write to me and ask whether it is possible at the moment.

How often are new works added to the website?

There is no fixed schedule, as each oil painting needs its own time for painting, drying and preparation. New series and pieces are added to the gallery as they are ready.

I like a work on the website but it is marked as sold. What can I do?

When a work is marked as sold, it already has an owner and is no longer available. If you really like the style or the series, you can email me to hear about future pieces or new releases.
