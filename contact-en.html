Shipping Information

Each artwork is shipped insured and individually packaged from the studio. Shipping costs vary by destination and are confirmed before the purchase is finalized:

  • Domestic shipping (mainland Spain): included in the price.
  • Shipping to islands: Balearic Islands, Canary Islands, Ceuta, and Melilla: please inquire.
  • International shipping: please inquire. Any customs duties are the responsibility of the buyer.
